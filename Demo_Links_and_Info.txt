   - Semantic Coherence: Sentence-BERT (MiniLM-L6-v2) for Headline-Body Consistency
   - Sentiment & Affective Dynamics: VADER Lexicon Polarity
   - Entity Recognition: spaCy Named Entity Extraction (PERSON, ORG, GPE, DATE)
